Singhanon

An offline Aklanon–English mobile dictionary prototype

Welcome to Singhanon, an offline Aklanon–English mobile dictionary prototype developed as part of a developmental research study. Singhanon is designed to provide an accessible digital reference for selected Aklanon words and their English meanings.

Through this prototype, Aklanon vocabulary is organized into a convenient mobile format while recognizing the importance of documenting and preserving the language.

Abu guid na saeamat for exploring Singhanon and for being part of the journey toward keeping the Aklanon language visible, accessible, and remembered.

Singhanon was developed to provide an accessible offline reference for selected Aklanon words and their English translations. It aims to organize Aklanon vocabulary into a convenient digital format that can be used for language reference, learning, and documentation.

As a research prototype, Singhanon also aims to demonstrate how existing Aklanon linguistic resources can be transformed into a functional mobile dictionary and evaluated for functionality, usability, and user acceptability.

  • Open the Dictionary tab and type a word in the search bar to look it up in Aklanon or English.
  • Use the letter strip to browse entries alphabetically.
  • Tap any word to see its full entry: part of speech, meaning, and an example sentence where available.
  • Tap the heart icon on a word to save it. Saved words appear under the Saved Words tab.
  • If you notice an entry that seems inaccurate or unsuitable for young learners, open the word and tap Report content.

This mobile dictionary prototype, Singhanon, was developed as part of a developmental research study entitled “Singhanon: A Developmental Research and Evaluation of an Offline Aklanon–English Mobile Dictionary Prototype.”

The study focuses on the development and evaluation of an offline digital dictionary that provides selected Aklanon words with their English translations and related information. It aims to create an accessible and user-friendly reference tool that can support the documentation and continued use of the Aklanon language.

The prototype undergoes evaluation to determine its functionality, usability, and acceptability among the intended users. Feedback gathered from the evaluation will be used to identify areas for improvement and guide the refinement of the prototype.

This application is a research prototype developed for academic purposes.

Source of Dictionary Entries

The Aklanon vocabulary and English translations presented in Singhanon are primarily based on A Study of the Aklanon Dialect, Volume Two: Dictionary (of Root Words and Derivations), Aklanon to English by Salas Reyes, Zorc, and Prado (1969).

The selected entries from the original dictionary were digitized and organized into an offline mobile dictionary prototype as part of this developmental research.
